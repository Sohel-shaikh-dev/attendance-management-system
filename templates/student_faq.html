Attendance
How is my attendance percentage calculated?
Your attendance percentage is calculated as: (Total classes attended ÷ Total classes held) × 100. This is calculated subject-wise and shown on your dashboard.
What is the minimum attendance required?
The minimum required attendance is 75% per subject. Students falling below this threshold are marked as defaulters and may not be allowed to appear in exams.
My attendance is marked wrong. What should I do?
If you believe your attendance has been incorrectly marked, please raise a support ticket with the category "Attendance". Include the date, subject, and lecture time in your message. The admin will review and correct it.
Can I see my attendance history?
Yes! Go to your Student Dashboard and you can view your complete attendance history, filter by subject, and see your attendance percentage for each subject.
Fees
How can I view my fee details?
Go to the "Fees" section in the sidebar. You can view your total fee, amount paid, due amount, and payment history for each semester.
How do I download my fee receipt?
In the Fees section, click on the "Download Receipt" button next to any payment entry. A PDF receipt will be generated and downloaded automatically.
My payment is not reflecting. What should I do?
If your payment is not showing in the system, please raise a support ticket with category "Fees". Attach your payment proof (transaction ID, bank receipt) in the message. Allow 2-3 working days for verification.
Exam
Will low attendance affect my exam eligibility?
Yes. Students with attendance below 75% in any subject may be declared ineligible to appear in the semester examination for that subject. Contact the admin immediately if you are at risk.
How do I get my exam hall ticket?
Exam hall tickets are issued by the college office. Ensure your fees are fully paid and attendance is above 75%. Contact the admin or raise a ticket if you face any issues.
Technical
I forgot my login password. How do I reset it?
Your login uses your registered Gmail ID and mobile number. If you've changed your mobile number, please contact the admin or raise a support ticket with category "Technical".
The portal is not loading properly. What should I do?
Try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then reload the page. If the issue persists, try a different browser or device. If still unresolved, raise a Technical support ticket.
How do I update my profile information?
Profile information (name, email, mobile) can only be updated by the admin. Please raise a support ticket with the correct details and the admin will update your profile.

Didn't find your answer?
