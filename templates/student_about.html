Our Story
Institute History & Ethos

Navneet College of Arts, Science & Commerce is a premier institution established under the aegis of the NES (Navneet Education Society), a trust committed to providing quality education to the youth of Mumbai since 1960. Located in the heart of Mumbai Central, the college has been a beacon of academic excellence and social responsibility for over six decades.

The college is affiliated to the University of Mumbai and has earned recognition from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) for its consistent commitment to quality education, infrastructure, and student welfare.

Rooted in the ethos of "Education for All", Navneet College has always prioritised accessibility, inclusivity, and academic rigour. The institution serves students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds, offering affordable yet world-class education in the heart of Mumbai.
